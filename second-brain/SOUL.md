# FitOps Project Identity

FitOps is a fictional gym class-booking and operations product built to demonstrate entry-level full-stack software-engineering capability.

The product should feel credible because its flows, rules, tests, decisions, and deployment are real. It must never imply that the fictional gym, users, revenue, customers, or metrics are real.

## Engineering character

- Plan before implementation.
- Prefer verifiable behavior over decorative claims.
- Keep business rules independent of frameworks.
- Use fictional and privacy-safe demo data.
- Document tradeoffs and rejected alternatives.
- Treat tests, accessibility, security, and deployment as product work.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity that cannot be justified by requirements.

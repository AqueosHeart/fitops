# Practice Athletic Club Logo Selection

## User goal

Choose the strongest logo direction for the fictional gym brand before any website or application work.

## Decision

- The user selected option 2 from the Mona Sans marker comparison.
- The approved system is a Mona Sans Display stacked wordmark (`wght 800`, `wdth 94`, `opsz 72`) with a short Signal lime `#C7F134` progress bar beneath `CLUB`.
- The primary identity is the full name, not a standalone icon. A compact P-plus-bar is limited to recognized contexts.
- Ink `#111310`, Bone `#F2F0E8`, and Signal lime are approved for logo behavior only. A complete accessible interface palette is still required.

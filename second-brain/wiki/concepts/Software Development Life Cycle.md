# Software Development Life Cycle

FitOps uses eight lifecycle phases: initiation and planning, requirements analysis, system and UX design, development, testing and quality assurance, deployment and release, operations and maintenance, and evaluation and retirement.

Sprints do not replace the lifecycle. They provide short delivery loops inside it. Testing, documentation, security, and accessibility remain continuous even when a later phase provides a dedicated system-level gate.
